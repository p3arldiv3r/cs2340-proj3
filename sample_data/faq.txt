Do I need to sign up for every program?
Some programs need a sign-up and some are drop-in. Each program’s page says which.

Is there a cost?
Most programs are free. A few have a small fee of $2 to $5 to cover supplies.

Can I bring a friend?
Yes! Guests are always welcome.
