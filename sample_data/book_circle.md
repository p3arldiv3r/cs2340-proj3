# Afternoon Book Circle: how it works

The Afternoon Book Circle meets online on the first Thursday of every month,
from 3:00 to 4:00 PM Eastern Time.

## Joining

You can join by video, audio only, or by phone. Captions are turned on for
every meeting. You do not need to have finished the book to take part.

## This month's book

Each month the group votes on the next book. Large-print and audio versions are
listed in the monthly email.
