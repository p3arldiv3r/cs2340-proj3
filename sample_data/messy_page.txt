Tech Help Hour &amp; Q&amp;A



<p>Bring your phone or tablet and get one‑on‑one help from a volunteer.</p>
Sessions run every Wednesday from 1:00 to 3:00 PM in the library’s community room.​

| | |
“I finally learned how to video call my grandkids,” says one regular.

What you can learn: making video calls, sharing photos, sending texts with pictures, spotting scam emails and phone calls, setting up larger text on your screen, and keeping your passwords safe. Volunteers go at your pace and will happily repeat any step. Nothing is too basic to ask about. If you do not have a device, the library has tablets you can borrow during the session. No sign-up is needed, but arriving in the first half hour helps us match you with a volunteer. The room is step-free and has a hearing loop. Parking is free in the library lot, and the number 12 bus stops right outside.
