Abstract

Objective

We tested whether a weekly online book group reduces loneliness in adults aged 65 and older.

Methods

Forty participants met by video once a week for eight weeks. Loneliness was measured before and after.

Results

Loneliness scores fell by an average of 12 percent. Participants said the regular schedule and small group size helped them feel connected.

Conclusion

Short, regular online groups are a promising, low-cost way to reduce loneliness.
